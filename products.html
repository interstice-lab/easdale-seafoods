Our Products

Live Langoustines (Nephrops norvegicus)

Overview

Scottish live langoustines—also called Dublin Bay prawns—are prized for their delicate sweet flavour and tender, succulent texture. Caught in creel pots off Scotland’s west coast, each is handled with care from sea to table.

Key Characteristics

Fishing Method
Creel pots—an eco-friendly approach minimising habitat impact.
Handling & Transport
Delivered live in insulated boxes with seawater gel packs.
Sizes
XXL, XL, L, M, S — Warmer months yield smaller catches.
Availability
Year-round
Traceability
Each box is linked to a specific boat for full provenance.
