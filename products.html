Our Products

Live Langoustines (Nephrops norvegicus)

Overview

Scottish live langoustines—also called Dublin Bay prawns—are prized for their delicate sweet flavour and tender, succulent texture. Caught in creel pots off Scotland’s west coast, each is handled with care from sea to table.

Key Characteristics

Fishing method
Creel-caught off Scotland’s west coast.
Packing
Shipped live with ice packs. Net weights and presentation depend on the grade, as shown below.
Availability
Supplied throughout the year. Quantities and grades vary with the season and fishing conditions; please confirm availability when enquiring.
Traceability
Each box carries a code identifying the fishing boat.

Razor Clams (Ensis siliqua)

Overview

Our razor clams are harvested by hand by divers. Their unique shape and flavour make them a favourite for our clients in Asia and Europe.

Key Characteristics

Harvesting method
Hand-dived.
Packing
22 kg boxes with gel packs for transport.
Grades
Please enquire about available grades.
Availability
Supplied through the year, with a spring fishing break. Availability depends on fishing periods and applicable restrictions; please confirm when enquiring.
Traceability
Each box carries a code identifying the fishing boat.

European Lobsters (Homarus gammarus)

Overview

Our European lobsters have sweet, succulent meat and a firm yet tender bite.

Key Characteristics

Packing
Variable-weight boxes, generally around 5–6 kg, with gel packs. A packing list gives the weight of each box.
Sizes
Various sizes, including 2 kg and over, subject to availability. Mixed-size boxes may be offered depending on available quantities.
Availability
Varies with landings. Please tell us your preferred size, quantity and destination when enquiring.
Traceability
Each box carries a code identifying the fishing boat.
