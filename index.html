About Us

Easdale Seafoods is a small, family-run business based on the West Coast of Scotland.

Since 1990, we have specialised in the export of live shellfish — with a focus on quality, sustainability and trust.

All our langoustines are caught in creel pots off Scotland’s west coast.

Our products are delivered to wholesalers, and distributors around the world — reflecting the care and consistency only a close-knit team can provide.
